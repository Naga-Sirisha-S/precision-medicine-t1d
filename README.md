# ML-Enabled Precision Medicine Approach Identifies MIF as a Key Immunological Biomarker and  TOP 10 Marine Natural Products as Therapeutic Leads in Type 1 Diabetes.
This study demonstrates the value of an integrated in silico strategy that combines transcriptomic analysis, machine learning, and structure-based drug discovery to identify clinically relevant biomarkers and candidate therapeutics in Type 1 Diabetes (T1D). Comprehensive analysis of whole-blood RNA-sequencing data, coupled with LASSO-based feature selection, identified macrophage migration inhibitory factor (MIF) as a promising immunological biomarker with strong diagnostic potential (AUC = 0.813). Virtual screening and molecular docking of marine-derived Aspergillus metabolites against MIF subsequently identified several high-affinity compounds, led by CMNPD30430, CMNPD23396, and CMNPD30428, as potential MIF inhibitors that formed favorable, stable protein–ligand interactions.
